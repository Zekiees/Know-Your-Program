## 🔎 Where you can use a Database in your site

Here are **features you could add** to make use of a database:

1. **User Accounts & Login** 🔐

   * Students can **sign up & log in**.
   * Database stores usernames, emails, and passwords (hashed).
   * Lets each user save their progress.

2. **Progress Tracking** 📊

   * Track how many lessons each student completed.
   * Store quiz scores or “badges” in the database.
   * Each student sees their own progress when they log in.

3. **Quizzes / Exercises** 📝

   * Store quiz **questions** and **answers** in the database.
   * Users answer quizzes → their responses get stored.
   * Later, you can even build a **leaderboard**.

4. **Comments / Discussions** 💬

   * Add a section under each lesson where students can leave comments.
   * Database stores the comment, username, and timestamp.

5. **Resources Library** 📚

   * Instead of hardcoding links, store them in a database.
   * Makes it easy to update/add new resources without editing HTML files.
