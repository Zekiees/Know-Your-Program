What You'll Learn

“Know Your Program” is an educational initiative designed to help students and beginners understand the fundamentals of various programming languages. Through interactive lessons, practical coding exercises, and real-world projects, learners can build confidence and fluency in the language of their choice, whether it's HTML, CSS , JavaScript, or more.
